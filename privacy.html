Zero Footprint Publisher – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Zero Footprint Publisher is a private tool used only by the owner of the Zero Footprint channel to publish the channel's own news videos to the owner's own social media accounts (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube).

Data we access

When the owner authorizes the tool, it receives an access token and basic account information (such as the account's open ID, display name and avatar) solely to publish videos to that same account and to confirm the publishing status.

Data we do not collect

The tool does not collect, sell or share data about any other users. It does not read followers, messages, or any content other than the videos it uploads.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and are used only to publish videos. They are never shared with third parties.

Deletion

The owner can revoke access at any time from the platform's app settings. Revoking access deletes the tool's ability to post. Questions: Instagram @0foot.print
